# Family Planning Helpline Data Structuring & Analysis
### DKT / Honey & Banana Connect | MEAL Analysis | 122 Transcripts

**Role:** Data Analyst (Upwork Contract - Client Work)  
**Period:** Nov-Dec 2024  
**Client:** DKT Nigeria via Honey & Banana Connect

### Problem
DKT had 122 raw helpline call transcripts (unstructured WhatsApp/voice notes) about family planning. Data was not usable for MEAL (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning) reporting - needed to extract demographics, product demand, knowledge level, decision factors.

### My Contribution
Structured raw transcripts into analyzable dataset + provided insights.

**Steps:**
1. Developed codebook for MEAL indicators
2. Coded 122 transcripts: Age, Location, Gender, Marital Status, Contraceptive Product (Postpill, Sayana, Condoms etc), Knowledge Level, Source of Info, Barriers
3. Built Excel dataset for analysis
4. Analyzed trends: Product Demand, Age distribution, Geographic spread, Misconceptions

### Key Results
- Total Records: 122 calls
- Top Demanded Products: Postpill (22), Injection (16), Implant (12), Condoms (10)
- Key Insight: 60% calls from 18-30yr, high demand for emergency contraception shows knowledge gap on regular methods
- Most reported barrier: Partner disapproval & fear of side effects
- Deliverable: Clean Excel dataset + Summary dashboard for MEAL team

### Why This Matters
Turned qualitative stories into quantitative evidence for program decisions. Helped DKT understand real demand for SRHR products to improve supply chain & counseling.
